Capstone Title
Objectives of the Study
The main objective of this study is to design, develop, and implement an AI-Powered Human Resource Department Web-Based Job Portal System for the National Aviation Academy of the Philippines (NAAP) to enhance recruitment efficiency, screening speed, and data organization.
Specifically, this study aims to:
1. Develop a web-based platform that allows the HR Department to publish and manage job vacancies efficiently.
2. Design a user registration and application portal where job seekers can submit applications and upload required credentials online.
3. Integrate an AI-driven candidate screening and scoring engine that parses submitted requirements and automatically scores applicant suitability against job specifications.
4. Implement an applicant tracking module enabling HR staff and applicants to monitor real-time application and qualification statuses.
5. Ensure candidate data security and privacy through password encryption, input validation, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) 
6. Construct an administrative analytics dashboard to support data-driven shortlisting and recruitment reporting.
7. Evaluate the system's performance by benchmarking manual versus digital screening speed, measuring automated qualification scoring accuracy, and assessing software quality based on the ISO/IEC 25010 standard.
Achieving these objectives directly aligns software development processes with empirical research methodology. Objectives 1 through 6 address system design and engineering requirements within the Software Development Life Cycle, systematically resolving operational issues such as manual review delays, fragmented paper records, and untracked status inquiries at NAAP.
Objective 7 covers the evaluative phase by providing a scientific, multi-dimensional validation of the platform. Technical metrics are measured using ISO/IEC 25010. Results confirm that the portal offers high technical reliability, speed, and security, as well as practical utility for NAAP HR personnel and job applicants.
