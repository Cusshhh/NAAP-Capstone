INTRODUCTION
               In the modern digital era, (the integration of web-based Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence (AI) into Human Resource Management (HRM) has evolved from an operational convenience into a strategic necessity.) [1], [4]. Traditional recruitment practices relying on paper-based workflows, manual record management, and human-intensive resume evaluation create (major) administrative bottlenecks, increase data redundancy, and prolong hiring timelines [2], [3], [6]. Manual resume screening is particularly prone to evaluator fatigue, inconsistent qualification verification, and subjective bias [5]. Modern empirical literature demonstrates that integrating machine learning algorithms and natural language processing (NLP) into web-based recruitment platforms (significantly) improves applicant screening accuracy, speeds up shortlisting, and enhances candidate data integrity across specialized public and educational institutions [3]â€“[5].
Specialized aviation institutions, such as the National Aviation Academy of the Philippines (NAAP), require highly structured and rigorous recruitment workflows [2]. HR personnel must evaluate specialized technical credentials, Civil Service eligibility, CAAP ratings, and specific academic background requirements across a growing volume of applicants [2]. NAAPâ€™s existing reliance on conventional semi-manual hiring mechanisms exposes its HR operations to misplaced documentation, untracked application statuses, and delayed candidate evaluation [6], [7]. While generic commercial Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) exist, recent research highlights that commercial tools are cost-prohibitive, lack customized AI algorithms aligned with specialized institutional job descriptions, and present data privacy concerns regarding external candidate hosting [2], [8], [11].
Statement of the Problem:
Despite the recognized capabilities of web-based e-recruitment platforms and AI screening technologies [1]â€“[5], NAAP continues to face critical operational friction due to the lack of a tailored, AI-enabled digital portal. Specifically, the HR Department faces three core challenges:
Inefficient Manual Candidate screening: (HR personnel spend excessive time manually reviewing resumes and verifying applicant qualifications, resulting in processing delays.) [5], [6], [8].
Fragmented Applicant Records: Reliance on physical filings and spreadsheets leads to misplaced documentation, data duplication, and lack of a unified database [3], [10].
Lack of Applicant Visibility: Candidates lack real-time visibility into their application and qualification status, causing high volumes of repetitive administrative inquiries [7].
Consequently, a distinct research problem exists regarding how an AI-powered web-based job portal system with automated candidate screening and scoring can systematically eliminate resume screening bottlenecks, standardize qualification scoring, and secure recruitment workflows for NAAP.

Purpose and Description
Purpose of the Study
This study exists to resolve the operational inefficiencies, manual screening delays, and data management vulnerabilities inherent in traditional recruitment workflows within public aviation educational institutions.
From an operational standpoint, the study modernizes NAAP's HR Department by deploying an intelligent digital platform that automatically screens, filters, and scores candidate applications based on submitted credentials and requirements.
From an academic and empirical standpoint, the study systematically measures and evaluates the impact of AI candidate screening on processing speed, filtering accuracy (Percentage Error), technical software quality (ISO/IEC 25010), and user adoption (Technology Acceptance Model).
Description of the System
The AI-Powered Human Resource Department Web-Based Job Portal System for National Aviation Academy of the Philippines is a browser-accessible platform built on a monolithic client-server architecture (Laravel/PHP backend, React/Inertia frontend, and MySQL database) integrated with an Automated Candidate Screening and Scoring Engine.
The system provides tailored, role-based environments for two primary user groups:
HR Administrators: Equipped with an administrative dashboard to manage job postings, define qualification criteria (education, certifications, experience), view AI-generated match scores and candidate rank lists, manage interview schedules, and export recruitment analytics.
Job Applicants: Provided with a responsive portal to explore open positions, submit digital application forms, upload resumes and requirement documents, and track their qualification and application status in real time.
The integrated AI Screening Module automatically parses uploaded applicant documents, compares extracted skills, education, and credentials against posted job specifications, and calculates a percentage suitability score [4], [5]. Security measures including password encryption, server-side data validation, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) safeguard sensitive applicant documentation [9]â€“[11].
Objectives of the Study
The main objective of this study is to design, develop, and implement an AI-Powered Human Resource Department Web-Based Job Portal System for the National Aviation Academy of the Philippines (NAAP) to enhance recruitment efficiency, screening speed, and data organization.
Specifically, this study aims to:
Develop a web-based platform that allows the HR Department to publish and manage job vacancies efficiently.
Design a user registration and application portal where job seekers can submit applications and upload required credentials online.
Integrate an AI-driven candidate screening and scoring engine that parses submitted requirements and automatically scores applicant suitability against job specifications [4], [5].
Implement an applicant tracking module enabling HR staff and applicants to monitor real-time application and qualification statuses [7].
Ensure candidate data security and privacy through password encryption, input validation, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) [9], [11].
Construct an administrative analytics dashboard to support data-driven shortlisting and recruitment reporting [8].
(Evaluate the developed system in terms of processing speed, screening accuracy, software quality (ISO/IEC 25010), and user acceptance (Technology Acceptance Model).)
Scope and Limitations
Scope
The research focuses on the design, development, and evaluation of an AI-powered web-based job portal for NAAPâ€™s Human Resource Department at the Villamor Air Base campus. The system scope includes user authentication, vacancy management, digital requirement upload, automated AI/ML candidate screening and qualification scoring, applicant shortlisting, real-time status tracking, and basic recruitment report generation. The software stack utilizes PHP/Laravel, JavaScript/React, and MySQL. System evaluation covers processing time comparison against manual screening, percentage error in document parsing/scoring, ISO/IEC 25010 software quality, and TAM usability testing with NAAP HR personnel and applicants.
Limitations
Document Format Dependency: The AI screening parser relies on readable digital document formats (e.g., standard PDF or DOCX text); image-based scanned PDFs lacking OCR quality may require manual verification [5], [10].
Scope of HR Functions: The system focuses exclusively on recruitment, application handling, automated screening, and initial shortlisting; it does not include post-hiring functions such as payroll, onboarding, or employee performance evaluation.
Network Infrastructure: The web portal requires an active internet connection for web access and server-side AI parsing.
AI Decision Support: The AI-based screening results serve only as decision-support recommendations and do not replace the final hiring decision made by the Human Resource Department. Final hiring decisions remain under the authority and professional judgment of the HR personnel.

System Development Framework

               The current recruitment process of the Human Resource Department of the National Aviation Academy of the Philippines (NAAP) is primarily conducted through manual and semi-digital methods. Job vacancies are typically announced through bulletin boards, emails, or external job platforms, while applicants submit their requirements through email or physical documents. HR personnel manually review submitted applications, organize applicant records, and track hiring progress using spreadsheets or paper-based files.

Evaluation of applicants is conducted by reviewing resumes and documents individually, which may lead to inconsistencies in decision-making due to the absence of a centralized and standardized system. The lack of automation limits the ability of HR personnel to efficiently filter, sort, and analyze applicant data.

Stakeholder Identification and User Roles

             The primary stakeholders of the system are the Human Resource (HR) Department of NAAP and the job applicants. The HR Department serves as the main user and decision-maker, responsible for managing job postings, reviewing applications, and monitoring applicant records. To support the recruitment workflow, the system is designed with two main user roles. The Admin (HR Personnel) has full access to the system, including managing job vacancies, reviewing applicant submissions, and updating application statuses. On the other hand, the Applicants interact with the system by registering accounts, browsing job opportunities, submitting applications, and tracking their application status. This role distribution ensures that both administrative and applicant-side processes are handled efficiently within a single platform.


Functional Requirements

             To ensure that the system meets the needs of the HR Department and applicants, the functional requirements are defined based on the recruitment process. The system includes a User Registration and Authentication function, which allows applicants and admin to securely access the system using login credentials. The Job Posting Management function enables the admin to create, update, and delete job vacancies. The Application Submission function allows applicants to apply for jobs and upload required documents such as resumes and other credentials. Additionally, the system includes an Application Tracking function, where applicants can monitor the status of their submitted applications. The Applicant Management function enables the admin to review, filter, and manage applicant data efficiently. Lastly, a Reporting function is included to provide summaries of applications and recruitment progress.
These functions ensure that all major recruitment activities are handled within the system.


Non-Functional Requirements

             The system must also meet non-functional requirements to ensure quality and performance. The first requirement is Security, which ensures that user data is protected through authentication and proper data validation. The system also follows basic data protection practices to safeguard applicant information. The second requirement is Usability, ensuring that the system provides a simple and user-friendly interface for both admin and applicants. Another requirement is Performance Efficiency, which ensures that the system responds quickly when loading pages, submitting applications, and retrieving data. The system must also be Reliable, meaning it should function consistently without errors during use. Lastly, Maintainability is considered, allowing the system to be easily updated or improved in the future.





Proposed System Architecture and Design

             The proposed system is designed to support a web-based recruitment process using a structured and organized architecture. It follows a three-tier architecture, separating the system into presentation, application, and data layers. This separation ensures better performance, security, and maintainability of the system. The system focuses on providing a smooth interaction between users and the platform, allowing real-time processing of applications and data management. Unlike manual systems, this design ensures that all recruitment activities are handled digitally and efficiently.


System Architecture Overview

Client Architectural Framework

             Adopting a web-based client-server architectural model, the system provides a centralized and efficient recruitment platform for the Human Resource Department of the National Aviation Academy of the Philippines (NAAP). This architecture ensures a clear separation between the user interface, application logic, and data storage, allowing the system to remain modular, maintainable, and scalable. Unlike traditional manual recruitment processes, this model enables real-time interaction between applicants and the HR Department through a browser-based system. It also ensures that updates in the backend or database structure do not require major changes in the frontend design, supporting long-term system flexibility and maintainability.


I. (Frontend System)

             The Presentation Layer serves as the primary interface for both applicants and HR administrators. It is developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, providing a responsive and user-friendly environment for system interaction. This layer allows applicants to register accounts, browse job postings, submit applications, upload required documents, and track their application status. For the HR administrators, it provides access to an admin dashboard where job postings are managed, applicant data is reviewed, and recruitment activities are monitored. The frontend is designed to ensure usability and accessibility, enabling users to navigate the system efficiently without requiring technical expertise. It also ensures that all user interactions are properly structured before being sent to the backend for processing.


Il. Application Programming Interface Layer (Backend Processing)

             The Application Programming Interface (API) Layer serves as the core processing unit of the system. It is developed using PHP, which handles server-side logic and processes all requests coming from the frontend. This layer is responsible for managing key system operations such as user authentication, job posting management, application submission processing, file uploads, and application status updates. It ensures that all data transmitted between the frontend and database is validated, secure, and properly processed. By separating business logic from the user interface, this layer ensures system efficiency, security, and smooth data handling between users and the database.


Ill. (Database System)

             The Data Management Layer is responsible for the storage and organization of all system data. The system utilizes a MySQL relational database to store information such as user accounts, job postings, applicant records, uploaded documents, and application statuses. This layer ensures that all recruitment data is securely stored and can be retrieved efficiently when needed. It also maintains data consistency and integrity, allowing both applicants and HR administrators to access updated and accurate information in real time.

CHAPTER IV
RESEARCH METHODOLOGY, RESULTS, AND DISCUSSION

Research Design
This study utilizes a developmental-descriptive research design to evaluate the Human Resource Department Web-Based Job Portal System for the National Aviation Academy of the Philippines (NAAP). The developmental phase followed the Waterfall Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) to systematically build the platform through sequential phases of requirement analysis, design, and implementation. The descriptive-evaluative phase is then employed to assess the system's effectiveness compared to the previous manual operations, specifically measuring how well the portal improves recruitment efficiency and data organization.

Data Gathering Technique
Data was gathered through three primary methods to directly address the objectives of the study. First, a time-motion study was conducted to measure processing efficiency by recording the time required to sort and track 50 simulated job applications using the traditional manual paper method versus the new web-based portal. Second, system audit logs were examined to evaluate data accuracy by comparing physical applicant records against the digital entries over a simulated operational period. Finally, standardized questionnaires were administered to gather feedback on technical quality using the ISO/IEC 25010 standard and user adoption viability through the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM).

Statistical Treatment of Data
Specific mathematical tools were applied to interpret the gathered data and test the studyâ€™s hypothesis. A Paired Samples T-Test was employed to determine if there is a statistically significant difference in efficiency between the previous manual recruitment workflow and the web-based system. To measure data accuracy, Percentage Error was calculated to identify any discrepancies between physical applicant files and the centralized database records. Lastly, the Weighted Mean was utilized to analyze responses from the software evaluation questionnaires, quantifying the overall technical quality and user acceptance of the portal.



Population and Sampling Technique
The study utilized a purposive sampling technique to ensure that the evaluators possessed the necessary expertise and context for the system. The primary respondents for the usability evaluation included the HR staff of NAAP, as the system is specifically tailored to their departmental workflow. To ensure technical validity, a selected group of IT professionals evaluated the system's architecture and security protocols. For efficiency testing, a sample of 50 common recruitment transactionsâ€”such as posting a vacancy and processing a resumeâ€”was used to ensure statistical reliability when measuring system performance.

Result and Discussion
The findings from the implementation and testing of the system are categorized into key performance areas. The time-motion study revealed a significant reduction in application processing speed, moving from hours of manual sorting to near-instant digital filtering. The percentage error evaluation confirmed that the centralized database eliminated duplicated records and "lost" applications common in the paper-based system. Technical evaluation based on ISO/IEC 25010 showed high scores in Functional Suitability and Security, proving that the portal effectively handles sensitive applicant data while remaining reliable. Finally, the TAM results indicated that the HR department found the system highly useful and easy to navigate, confirming that the digital transformation successfully met the operational needs of the academy.
